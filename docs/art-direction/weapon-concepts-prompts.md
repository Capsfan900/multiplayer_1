# Standalone weapon concept prompts

Method: built-in ImageGen, one independent generation per weapon. These are new
standalone weapon images using the approved visual direction as a written brief;
no map or character image is edited. Four candidates, not an approved roster.

## W01 — Line Driver

Output: `W01-line-driver.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: ONE standalone fictional FPS arena weapon concept illustration, a landscape product-style concept card.
Art direction: belongs in Wildstone Modern, a sophisticated stylized FPS with pale modernist environments and simple human silhouettes. Weapons have their own expressive science-fiction design. Render this weapon as polished stylized 3D concept art: broad clean forms, graphic shadow shapes, very subtle selective edge contours, satin ivory and dark graphite surfaces, restrained mechanical detail, a small functional color accent. Attractive game-like finish, not photorealistic, not gritty military realism, not a child's foam toy.
Presentation: one large fully visible weapon centered on a quiet light warm-gray studio backdrop with a soft grounding shadow; three-quarter side view at a slight angle so both profile and emission direction read clearly. Entire silhouette and handle visible, generous margins, weapon occupies most of the frame. Crisp clean title at upper left and short subtitle below. No other text. No environment, no characters, no human hands, no FPS HUD, no comparison grid, no bonus weapons, no logos, no watermark, no blueprints or manufacturing dimensions. Ergonomic-looking grip and clear emission direction, but entirely fictional mechanism. No animal features, stone ruins, wood, gems, vines, ornament or fantasy decorations. Each design must be a distinct recognizable tool, not a conventional rifle repainted.

Weapon design: A long, slim angular split-rail blaster: two parallel ivory prongs with a deliberate open slot of negative space between them, narrow graphite focusing core, an offset compact rear capacitor above a crisp pistol grip, minimal skeletal shoulder brace. The long narrow front and small crisp body contrast with bulky launchers. Use a few cyan light slits inside the rails to show the shot path; rail tips distinctly open and not joined into a normal barrel. One subtle cyan charge filament suspended between the rails, no giant blast or beam obscuring the form. Elegant fast precision instrument with a memorable asymmetrical silhouette; not a sniper rifle with a scope, no conventional magazine. Show facing toward the upper right.
Text (verbatim): "W01 / LINE DRIVER" and "PRECISION / SPLIT-RAIL BLASTER".
Critical: show exactly one complete weapon, isolated and unobscured, with the specific distinctive silhouette described.
```
