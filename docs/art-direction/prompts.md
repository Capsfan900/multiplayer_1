# FPS arena style comparison prompts

Method: built-in image generation. Three original concept images; not Unity renders
and not performance evidence. All use first-person perspective, a common scene brief,
and VHOLUME's monumental architecture as a reference. Final style not selected.

Reference: [VHOLUME on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4131730/VHOLUME/).
The locally saved official demo screenshot is reference material, not our game asset.

## A / GRAPHIC BRUTALISM

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: simple art-direction preview of an original first-person multiplayer parkour arena, not a logo or marketing illustration.
Create one wide 16:9 landscape 3D gameplay-like still, clearly labeled as concept art. Inspired by the monumental brutalist concrete architecture and layered parkour routes of VHOLUME on Steam, made more stylized and cartoon-like. This is an original game, do not copy an actual screenshot.
Fixed common scene for comparing three styles: eye-level first-person view at 1.7m with a normal wide gaming lens, looking across a compact open-air concrete courtyard. Low broad steps foreground lead down toward a circular capture zone painted on the flat floor. A low thin cyan ring with a few direction chevrons marks the moving objective; no transparent dome. A chunky weapon pickup rests on a low central pedestal just beyond the ring. Tall slab architecture and a huge circular industrial opening form the background; a raised catwalk crosses to the right, a short ramp joins it, and a broad wall-run surface sits to the left. One blue teammate with a small diamond marker and one coral opponent with a triangle marker are clearly visible at mid distance, simplified sporty human characters in original geometric suits with readable limbs, not military realism, not cute mascots. The foreground right shows a modestly sized original chunky energy launcher and gloved hand, taking less than 12% of the frame. Same overall scene contents and viewpoint in all style variations.
Designed for a fast competitive 8-player shooter: clear flat navigable surfaces, believable player scale, strong silhouette hierarchy, simple modular geometry, open sight lines, a tiny white crosshair, no distracting decorative clutter. The look should plausibly come from economical real-time rendering: one sun, baked soft fill/contact shading, mostly opaque matte surfaces, few material families, very restrained emissive details. No dense particles, volumetric lighting, ray-traced reflections, photoreal PBR microdetail, motion blur, film grain, depth of field, dense foliage, floating text spam, UI statistics or fake FPS numbers.
Polished art direction, aesthetically striking, legible as an actual playable space rather than an isometric miniature. Text only a small clear title in upper left and a small unobtrusive lower-left label "ART DIRECTION CONCEPT".
Style A: angular low-poly graphic brutalism. Flat chamfers, sharply faceted silhouettes, two-tone cel-shaded sunlight, broad warm concrete-gray planes with subtle sparse painted wear, charcoal shadows, muted sage accents and small vivid tangerine route marks. Pale overcast mint sky. Dry matte material, crisp yet calm, slightly retro minimalist urban dystopia. Very little texture noise, no black outlines. Geometry feels architectural and monumental, not toy-like. Strong value separation makes the two players and cyan objective stand out. This is the closest option to VHOLUME's restrained concrete identity, but visibly more graphic and stylized.
Title text exactly: "A / GRAPHIC BRUTALISM".
